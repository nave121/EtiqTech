## Risks
- **Riskiest: the regex (step 2).** A pattern that matches too much passes weak monitoring again; one that matches too little floods approved protocols with warnings. Blast radius: every severity ≥4 report, and merging to `main` deploys. Mitigation: the firing table on every real export must equal the chosen row in spec.md, and a 25-phrase self-check covers the edge words (`יומיים`, `כל שבועיים`, every 10 days, every 12h).
- **Default gate (step 2).** An error where a warning was decided would fail approved protocols. Mitigation: `batch_process.py examples/known-good --fail-on-error` and the per-export status diff in both profiles.
- **Test and manifest edits (steps 3, 4).** F1 and F2 are decided (D4); the diff must show no other test or manifest change.

## Not chosen
- **Stripping once-weekly phrases before searching.** Tried in scratch: "every two weeks" (`כל שבועיים`) then passed while weekly failed. The regex simply leaves once-weekly out.
- **A separate warning tier for "nothing every 48 h"** (V2 as advisory). More code, and Razy declined V2.
- **Moving the regex to `helpers.py`.** One rule uses it.
