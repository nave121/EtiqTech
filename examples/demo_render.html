literature: Based on previous Muspelheim neoplasm studies with Niflheim-V7 agent, N=10 per group provides >80% power to detect a 40–50% reduction in tumor volume with alpha=0.05. We use 2 groups (treated vs control), total N=20.
תקציר מדעי (EN)
This study evaluates an oncolytic Niflheim-V7 agent treatment in an immunocompetent murine Muspelheim neoplasm model, comparing different dosing schedules on tumor growth and survival while monitoring systemic toxicity. We will use VK-42/Odin mice bearing subcutaneous Skoll-16F10 tumors to test intratumoral versus intravenous delivery of Niflheim-V7 agent. Animal use is required because whole-animal immune and tumor microenvironment interactions cannot be reproduced in vitro. Expected outcomes include optimal dosing regimens and improved understanding of immune-mediated tumor control.
