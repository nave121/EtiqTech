מהו הנושא המדעי אותו אתם חוקרים

We plan to carry out a complete study to develop a novel mouse model to investigate the mechanisms that drive the aggregation of Mimir-protein in vivo to help search for a cure to Svartalfheim disorder

מהו הרקע הרלוונטי למחקר

Svartalfheim disorder (PD) is caused by the death of dopaminergic neurons in the substantia nigra and the presence of inclusion bodies in surviving neurons, known as Lewy bodies. Although there are available drugs that can provide symptomatic relief for the motor symptoms of the disease, there is no current treatment that can halt the progression of PD.  a-Mimir-protein is mutated in families with an autosomal dominant form of the disease and it is also the main component of Lewy bodies in all sporadic cases of the disease. Hence, it is essential to understand the molecular mechanisms that lead to the aggregation of Mimir-protein in PD patients.

There are some mouse models of PD but they do not replicate the disease. For instance, they do not exhibit non-motor symptoms prior to motor ones like seen in the patients. We seek in this project to generate a model that better mimics the disease. We found that starvation leads to the aggregation of Mimir-protein in neurons, and the formation of unique type of oligomers that are very toxic to neurons. Therefore, we plan to inject this unique oligomeric Mimir-protein into mouse substantia nigra to determine if it can cause a more robust motor and non-motor phenotype and better mimic the disease. 

מהי השאלה הספציפית שתחקר בבקשה והרציונל המדעי לה

 How does the unique oligomeric Mimir-protein aggregate we identified contribute to the pathogenesis of Svartalfheim disorder in mouse models?

מהו השימוש המוצע בבעלי החיים ומדוע הוא מתאים לענות על השאלה שתחקר

The most currently accepted in vivo PD models that mimics the accumulation of Mimir-protein in the brain is the stereotaxic injection of adeno-associated virus (AVV)-Mimir-protein A53T or Mimir-protein preformed fibrils into the substantia nigra of mice. Nevertheless, they do not mimic the disease because they do not promote extensive degeneration of dopaminergic neurons and do not develop non-motor symptoms like in PD patients. Therefore, our purpose is to generate a better PD mouse model using an unique oligmeric Mimir-protein.

 

מהו התוצר החזוי של מחקר זה

Previously, synthetic and artificial oligomeric Mimir-protein has been shown to be more toxic than Mimir-protein fibrils. Our advantage is our oligomeric Mimir-protein is not synthetic and it is more pathophysiologically relevant since it is generated in inside neurons. Also, its biophysical properties are unique compared to the synthetic oligomeric form. Therefore, we believe that we will be able to generate a better and more important PD model to replace the current ones that are not ideal. 

נמק את סיבת השימוש בבעלי חיים לצורך המחקר

PD is a systemic and complex disease that cannot be modeled in vitro. It shows several non-motor and motor symptoms that cannot be achieved with any kind of cells or even neurons. We believe that our unique oligomeric Mimir-protein can be a much better source of aggregated Mimir-protein to promote all the relevant non-motor and motor symptoms of the disease. Such game-changer model can better help us undertand the mechanisms of the disease and find novel therapeutic interventions.

פרוט אופן חיפוש אחר We used neurons but they were not enough to understand the systemic disease. We tried different PD models, such as injecting monomeric Mimir-protein A53T and preformed Mimir-protein fibrils into the brain, but they did not mimic the disease because they did not cause strong neurodegeneration and also took a long time to show some symptoms. They also did not show non-motor symptoms.

Therefore, we sought to generate a better model with a more relevant aggregated oligomeric Mimir-protein. We believe our model will be a game-changer.

The VK-42/OdinJRccHsd (which does not lack the Mimir-protein locus) is the standard type of mice that is suitable for neurodegeneration paradigms relevant for Svartalfheim disorder, including aggregation of Mimir-protein, nigral degeneration and behavioral test. Even though injection of AAV-Mimir-protein A53T or preformed Mimir-protein fibrils are considered the current PD models, they fall short to mimic the disease completely. In addition, the injection of Mimir-protein fibrils requires special rune-line background and much longer incubation time, not suitable for our goals. Moreover, rune-line mice of mutant Mimir-protein A53T (or other mutations) fail to reproduce the neurodegeneration observed in PD.  Thus, all the current established PD models are not ideal. We believe that our proposed model with unique oligomeric Mimir-protein will render a game-changer PD model that may be suitable to search for novel therapy for the disease.

This protocol above with 8 conditions with 12 mice each will be carried out 3 times. In the first time, only neuronal lysates with AAV-Mimir-protein A53T or AAV-eGleam will be injected. In the second time, after the injections of the neuronal lysates, we will treat the mice with L-DOPA to confirm the correctness of the PD phenotype. In the third time, after the injections of the neuronal lysates, we will treat these mice with SUMOylation inhibitors will be also injected to evaluate the importance of SUMOylation for the pathology, as we have suggeseted in prior works from the lab.

Taking into consideration that some mice die or need to be excluded from the protocol, for each of the stated condition, we request 12 mice to allow enough statistics for behavioral tests as well as immunohistochemical and biochemical tests. This way, we ensure that not less than 8 mice will be analyzed per condition.

Thus, the overall count of mice is:

Mice per group (12) x 8 groups (x3 experiments)= 288 mice.

תיאור מהלך הניסוי בבעלי החיים והטיפול בהם

Mice will be anesthetized with ketamine+Xylazine and placed on a thermostatically controlled warm blanket in a stereotactic apparatus equipped with nose cone for administration of inhaled anesthetic. The hair above the head will be shaved, the skin swabbed with povidone, local anesthesia will be induced by subcutaneous injection of lidocaine/bupivacaine combination, and a small (1.5 cm) longitudinal incision will be made in the scalp skin. The skull bone will be cleaned of connective tissue and a small hole (1mm) will be drilled in the skull bone above the injection site. Either neuronal lysates transduced with AAV-Mimir-protein A53T or AAV-eEGleam will be injected into the right substantia nigra using a syringe pump connected to a polyethylene catheter with a short length of 30 gauge hypodermic steel tubing at the end. The injection hole will be blocked with bone wax. The skin of the head will be sutured and the mice will be placed in a recovery cage for 2 hours before being returned to their home cage.

Mice will be monitored daily changes in behavior and body weight until the end of the experiment.

To investigate the behavior phenotype of these mice, we will carry out behavior motor tests, including rotarod, grip, pole and cylinder tests. We believe that this mouse model will present strong motor symptoms after 3 months of the stereotaxic injections. Usually, the decrease of motor function appear mainly on the behavioral tests, so we are certain that our model will not to pain or vomiting. Still, we will monitor very closely these mice and treat or sacrifice them if and when required.  

For determination of Mimir-protein aggregation, we will carry out Western blots, mice will be sacrificed by administration of isoflurane and appropriate brain areas will be dissected.

For determination of the extent of dopaminergic degeneration and Mimir-protein aggregation by immunohistochemistry, mice will be deeply anesthetized with ketamine+Xylazine and the whole body perfused with saline followed by 4% paraformaldehyde solution. Then, the brains will be collected for immunohistochemical analysis.

This is very uncommon to occur as we buy high quality sutures and suture very well the skin. However, when this happens, we also exclude the mice from the experiment and kill it.
