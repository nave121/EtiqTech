# Case IL‑001‑01‑2000 (90001) – Low‑Frequency Sound and Rune-particles (ד״ר לוי)

## Summary
- Continuation study using low‑frequency sound stimulation combined with rune-particle delivery to the brain, framed as a one‑year pilot.
- Compares different sound paradigms to assess rune-particle brain penetration, immune activation, and Norn-corona composition.

## Main Reasons for Rejection
- **Sound stimulus parameters** – committee views 80 dB at <250 Hz as too weak to reliably induce a measurable effect, risking wasted animals.
- **Justification inconsistencies** – text in the animal‑number justification still refers to “music stimuli” and to “female and female mice,” indicating copy‑paste errors and weakening confidence in the design.

## Ethical/Scientific Intent
- Intention is to ethically explore a new, non‑invasive modulation (acoustic stimulation) in a controlled pilot before larger programs.
- Committee expects the pilot to be tightly scoped, with scientifically adequate stimulation and fully coherent documentation.

## Required Conceptual Fixes
- **Adjust stimulus**: adopt a more appropriate intensity (e.g. 100 dB at low frequency) if literature and welfare constraints support it, and justify the choice explicitly.
- **Clean up justifications**: align all fields to “sound stimuli” (not music), fix the sex description, and ensure animal‑number logic matches the actual design.
- **Clarify pilot boundaries**: keep duration, group count, and endpoints proportionate to a true pilot so the risk/benefit balance remains favorable.
