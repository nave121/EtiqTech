# Case IL‑003‑01‑2000 (90003) – Multimodal Imaging of Midgard vascular Events (ד״ר כהן)

## Summary
- New study combining Midgard vascular event induction in mice with advanced imaging to follow Midgard vascular events over time.
- Uses anesthesia, analgesia, and serial imaging to track lesion development and recovery.

## Main Reasons for Rejection
- **Euthanasia inconsistency** – body text discusses perfusion under anesthesia, while the euthanasia field lists only cervical dislocation; the combination and sequence are not clearly specified.
- **Hidden monitoring details** – key information (such as the animal tracking table and some endpoint logic) exists only in attached files, not in the main request.

## Ethical/Scientific Intent
- Aim is to align imaging endpoints with clinical Midgard vascular event questions while respecting high‑burden classification (pain grade 4).
- To approve such a protocol, the committee needs unambiguous, self‑contained information about when and how animals are euthanized.

## Required Conceptual Fixes
- **Explicit two‑step euthanasia**: clearly define a sequence such as “isoflurane anesthesia followed by cervical dislocation,” and reflect it consistently in all fields.
- **Inline welfare description**: summarize monitoring frequency, scoring, and humane endpoints in the main form, using attachments only as supplementary documentation.
