# Case IL‑005‑03‑2000 (90005) – Gjallarhorn-9/Gjallarhorn-10 Chemokine Immunotherapy (ד״ר גרין)

## Summary
- New HR-7/Frigg tumor model testing mono and combined therapy of anti‑Gleipnir-1 with stabilized chemokines Gjallarhorn-10‑Ig and Gjallarhorn-9‑Ig.
- Includes multiple tumor models (e.g. SC, Fenris-26), treatment combinations, and survival endpoints.

## Main Reasons for Rejection
- **Sex selection justification** – initial restriction to female mice lacked a clear, data‑driven rationale; committee worried this could force a later male study and duplicate animal use.
- **Group labeling errors** – treatment tables repeated the same group labels (e.g. Gjallarhorn-10‑Fc + anti‑Gleipnir-1) instead of clearly distinguishing Gjallarhorn-10 vs Gjallarhorn-9 combinations.
- **Endpoint and euthanasia clarity** – committee requested explicit addition of a secondary euthanasia method (cervical dislocation) and clear linkage between tumor burden, clinical signs, and sacrifice.

## Ethical/Scientific Intent
- The study aims to refine immunotherapy regimens while controlling group sizes and tumor burden.
- To be ethical, it must convincingly minimize unnecessary groups (e.g. by choosing one sex) and define sharp humane endpoints.

## Required Conceptual Fixes
- **Sex choice**: provide concrete lab data and references supporting single‑sex use (e.g. more homogeneous tumors in females) and show that this reduces animal numbers.
- **Clean group definitions**: ensure each experimental arm has a unique, correct label and matching description across all sections.
- **Euthanasia and endpoints**: tie specific tumor sizes, ulceration, and clinical signs directly to euthanasia actions (CO₂ plus cervical dislocation) in the main form.
