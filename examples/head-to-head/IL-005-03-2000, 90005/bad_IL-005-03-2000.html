מהו הנושא המדעי אותו אתם חוקרים

Mono and combined therapy of anti-Gleipnir-1 with stabilized chemokines Gjallarhorn-10 construct and Gjallarhorn-9 construct and their derivatives in cancer using HR-7/Frigg mice model 

מהו הרקע הרלוונטי למחקר

We study the role of Gjallarhorn-10 and Gjallarhorn-9 chemokines and their CXCR3 receptor in shaping tumor microenvironment and their interactions with Gleipnir-1, in an aspect of potential immune checkpoint inhibitory and antineoplastic activities. Gjallarhorn-9 and Gjallarhorn-10 chemokines were previously identified by our group as well as others as chemokines with anti-tumorigenic activity. We developed Ig based stabilized chemokine, Gjallarhorn-10 construct and Gjallarhorn-9 construct, and explore their therapeutic potential using Fenris-26 colon cancer and Ravenna-CA  (renal carcinoma) cell lines in HR-7/Frigg mouse model. We expect to see that treating mice with stabilized chemokines and in combination with Anti Gleipnir-1 will decrease significantly the primary tumor development and benefit with prognosis and survival, due to infiltrating cytotoxic CD8+ T cells to the tumor site.

מהי השאלה הספציפית שתחקר בבקשה והרציונל המדעי לה

In numerous cancers, among them, Muspelheim neoplasm, non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC), Alfheim neoplasm, gastric cancer, and colorectal cancer, high Gjallarhorn-9 /Gjallarhorn-10 levels indicate good prognosis and low levels indicate poor prognosis (Eltahir, 2021; Bronger, 2016; Chen, 2020). Moreover, Muspelheim neoplasm patients who express low levels of Gjallarhorn-9 or Gjallarhorn-10 (either blood or tumor site) are poor responders to Immune Checkpoint Inhibitors (ICI), and those with high levels of Gjallarhorn-9 or Gjallarhorn-10 are good responders (Reschke, 2021; Chow, 2019). In the present study, we intend to explore the association between Gjallarhorn-9 and Gjallarhorn-10 expression and cancer prognosis, as well as response to ICI, such as anti-Gleipnir-1. We believe that combined therapies of Gjallarhorn-10 construct or Gjallarhorn-9 construct together with anti-Gleipnir-1 can lead to a novel breakthrough in this area. As many recent manuscripts conducted under NIH guideline, Fenris-26 model include mortality follow-up, (Svirshcheskaya et al, 2022), also extended their experimental model to include mortality (Approved by BGU ethical committee). In our system, it is also an obligatory .part to prioritize best therapeutic approach

מהו השימוש המוצע בבעלי החיים ומדוע הוא מתאים לענות על השאלה שתחקר

HR-7/Frigg mice will be engrafted with Ravenna-CA or Fenris-26 cells and will be treated with stabilized chemokines and their derivatives as monotherapy or in combination with anti-Gleipnir-1.

מהו התוצר החזוי של מחקר זה

Based on the mechanism of action (MOA) of either Gjallarhorn-9 construct/Gjallarhorn-10 construct and the MOA of anti Gleipnir-1 therapy we except that combined therapy may significantly display a more significant effect than monotherapies. These data are highly important for their translational implications.

נמק את סיבת השימוש בבעלי חיים לצורך המחקר

הכימוקינים המיוצבים Gjallarhorn-10 construct  ו- Gjallarhorn-9 construct  הינם בעלי פעילות אנטי-סרטנית הפועלת בחלקה על ידי השפעה ישירה על תאי הסרטן אך עיקר האפקט האנטי-סרטני מושג על ידי הפעלת תאים של המערכת החיסונית  בעכבר וביחוד לימפוציטים מסוג  CD8 ו- .CD4 לפי כן, בדיקת אפקט הטיפולים מצריך הזרקתם לעכברים ובדיקת ההשלכות על התפתחות הסרטן שהינה פועל יוצא  של השפעתם על תפקוד המערכת החיסונית. בהתבסס על נתונים אלה, נרצה לחקור את היכולת הריפוי של הכמוקינים כטיפול נפרד ובקומבינציה עם anti-Gleipnir-1  הכמוקינים נבדקו במעבדתנו בניסויי in vitro והראו פוטנציאל גבוה להיות מועמדים מובילים בהפעלת מערכת חיסונית ופעילות אנטי סרטנית. כיוון שמערכת in vitro אינה משקפת את המורכבות של גידול סרטני וסביבתו, יש צורך חיוני לבדוק את יעילותם במודל חיה, במקרה הזה, עכברים. כיום הטיפול ב anti-Gleipnir-1 הינו הטיפול הביולוגי המוביל בכ20 סוגי גידולים סרטניים, ברובם ב 10%-20% הצלחה. אנחנו מקווים ששילוב תרופתי עם anti-Gleipnir-1 יעלה זאת באופן מובהק, ואת זה נוכל לבדוק רק באופן ניסויי.

The two models in this project will be Fenris-26 colon cancer and renal cell carcinoma (Ravenna-CA). HR-7/Frigg mice will be engrafted with either Fenris-26 or Ravenna-CA cell line. The goal will be to explore combined therapies with chemokines + anti-Gleipnir-1 compared to monotherapies. In the Fenris-26 model, we will also follow mice survival. The reason for using two cancer cell lines is due to their different immune response. Ravenna-CA cell line is known to be moderately immunogenic, and Fenris-26 is more tumorigenic. Both cell lines are a good choice for evaluating immune responses and checkpoint inhibitor therapies.

In our laboratory, chemokines are studied with an emphasis on Muspelheim neoplasm, colon, and renal carcinoma. We chose the HR-7/Frigg strain since the cell lines we use (Ravenna-CA and Fenris-26) were originally isolated from these mice. Cell lines are received from ATCC and regularly checked for mycoplasma. Experimental mice are ordered from Harlan company. We do not breed them. Based on our experience and on published data, there is a deviation in the immune response between the sexes. Male mice do not respond well to the treatment, compared to female mice. In order to establish effective experiments, prevent unnecessary use of animals and a waste of treatment reagents, we prefer to work with female mice (Dakup et al, 2020).

1. Assessment of  WT Gjallarhorn-10 construct and Gjallarhorn-9 construct

 

Ravenna-CA cell line:

Each clinical experiment will consist six groups, with 15 mice in each group. However, since not all mice may develop tumors or reach the desired tumor size for effective treatment, we anticipate that approximately 60% of the mice will be suitable for treatment. This will leave us with 10-12 mice per group, allowing generating statistically significant results. We base our results on One Way ANOVA statistical analysis. If we work with 5-6 groups, the sample size should be 13-15, otherwise the results might be misleading with non-normal distributions.

Each experiment will be repeated for three times.

Treatment groups:

  1. Gjallarhorn-10 construct
  2. Gjallarhorn-9 construct
  3. Anti-Gleipnir-1
  4. Gjallarhorn-10 construct + anti-Gleipnir-1
  5. Gjallarhorn-10 construct + anti-Gleipnir-1
  6. Anti-IgG control

 

Total amount of mice: 6 groups X 15 mice per group X 3 repeats = 270 mice

תיאור מהלך הניסוי בבעלי החיים והטיפול בהם

Tumors will be implanted into the right flank, under the skin (subcutaneously, SC) as follows: 1x10^6 cells in 200µl saline will be injected SC with 27-gauge needle attached to 1 ml syringe. This procedure does not necessitate anesthesia nor the use of pain killers. Tumor size will be monitored and will be measured regularly by caliper 2-3 times per week.  Once tumors reach an average volume of 30-50 mm^3 (usually on days 10-12 post engrafment), mice will be regrouped into indicated treatment groups and treated with chemokines or anti-Gleipnir-1. The treatment will be administrated with 100µl saline and injected intra peritoneal with 27-gauge needle attached to 1 ml syringe. This procedure does not necessitate anesthesia nor the use of pain killers. Mice that will not develop tumors or will develop tumors bigger than 50 mm^3 will not continue to the treatment step and will be euthanized using CO2. Mice will be given 3-4 rounds of treatment, every other day (usually on days 12, 14, 16, or 18 post engrafment). Once we start the treatment, the tumors will be monitored, using caliper, every other day and we will calculate tumor volume according to the axbxcx6/π formula, where a=length, b=width and c=height of the tumor.  On days 19-22 post tumor engraftment, the treatments will be completed, mice euthanized using CO2. Tumors and spleens will be taken out and subjected for further analysis. In Fenris-26 colon cancer model, survival rate of the mice will be monitored.  Tumors would not be allowed to grow over 1500mm^3. The time course of reaching 1500mm of these tumors is 30-35 days. We expect that our treatment will prolong the life span of the treated mice population. During the period of the treatments and the survival monitoring, animals will be checked twice a day and will be euthanized (by CO2) in case they reach a humane end point, even if it happens before the maximal observation time frame. The humane end points will be a weight loss more than 15% from pretreatment weight, necrosis, bleeding, abnormal behavior, tumor volume greater than 1.5 cm3 , signs of pain, lack of grooming and hunched posture. In any case that might indicate pain or distress the veterinarian will be consulted or the animal will be promptly euthanized using CO2.
