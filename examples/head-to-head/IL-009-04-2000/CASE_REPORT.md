# Case IL‑009‑04‑2000 – Localized Translation in the Heart (קהת)

## Summary
- Study of localized translation and hypertrophic response in adult rat cardiomyocytes.
- Uses systemic administration of hypertrophic stimulators (phenylephrine, isoproterenol, AngII) and subsequent cell isolation for SINAPS imaging.

## Main Reasons for Rejection
- **Dose and welfare justification** – committee asks how doses of phenylephrine, isoproterenol, and AngII were selected and what their expected welfare impact is.
- **Analgesia omission** – for at least one experiment (experiment 6), some discomfort is expected but no analgesics are planned.

## Ethical/Scientific Intent
- Scientifically, the goal is to map translational control in cardiomyocytes under stress, which is hard to model without in vivo hypertrophic stimuli.
- Ethically, systemic stimulation must be balanced by careful monitoring and pain mitigation where discomfort is likely.

## Required Conceptual Fixes
- **Literature‑backed dosing**: cite specific references or pilot data linking chosen doses to hypertrophy with acceptable morbidity, and describe anticipated signs (tachycardia, distress).
- **Analgesia policy**: either introduce analgesia for the relevant arms or provide a strong, explicit rationale (e.g. very brief, mild procedures where analgesics would confound outcomes).
- **Monitoring plan**: describe clinical observations (behavior, weight, respiratory signs) and thresholds for early euthanasia in animals showing adverse reactions.
