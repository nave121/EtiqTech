# Case IL‑013‑05‑2000 (90013) – Bifrost nerve compression Neuroprotection (ד״ר כהן)

## Summary
- Mouse Bifrost nerve compression (BNC) model testing neuroprotective treatments (Valhalla-PA, Bifrost-Q10, Asgard oxygen protocol) vs control.
- Includes multiple groups, three time points (days 1, 3, 21), and histological and molecular readouts.

## Main Reasons for Rejection
- **Generic humane endpoints** – early versions reuse a broad list (anorexia, pulmonary edema, etc.) that clearly comes from a different systemic model and does not match BNC‑specific risks.
- **Iterative welfare corrections** – committee repeatedly requested tailored endpoints and conditions for euthanasia specific to ocular surgery and BNC, yet remnants of generic wording persisted.

## Ethical/Scientific Intent
- Intention is to maximize information about neuroprotection per animal (multiple time‑points, rich analyses) while maintaining strict welfare controls.
- Committee accepts the scientific rationale but wants the welfare language to be clearly anchored in this exact model.

## Required Conceptual Fixes
- **Model‑specific endpoints**: rewrite humane endpoints to focus on ocular complications (corneal ulceration, infection), weight loss, severe pain scores, and neurological signs relevant to BNC.
- **Coherent anesthesia/analgesia**: keep the ketamine/xylazine plus topical anesthetic regimen and buprenorphine schedule clearly documented and consistent across all sections.
- **Ethical framing**: emphasize reduction (shared controls, justified N) and refinement (pain scoring, early euthanasia) in the narrative, not only in annexes.
