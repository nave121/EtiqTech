# Case IL‑021‑07‑2000 (90021) – Multiscale Immune‑Vascular Mapping in Ageing and Jotunheim metabolic condition (ונדורנה)

## Summary
- Large, long‑term program combining intravital microscopy, PET/SPECT/CT, and MRI to study how ageing and Jotunheim metabolic condition alter bone‑marrow, brain, and heart immune‑vascular interactions.
- Uses multiple strains (VK-42/Odin and rune-lines), very large animal numbers, and extended follow‑up (up to 30 months).

## Main Reasons for Rejection
- **Strain and GM classification errors** – wild‑type VK-42/Odin mice were incorrectly marked as genetically modified.
- **Housing realism and welfare** – aged mice often become aggressive and must be singly housed, increasing cage numbers and potentially altering behavior; this was not properly considered.
- **Sex and group design** – text suggests both sexes, but group definitions assign a single sex per genetic background without rationale.
- **Unrealistic ages and N** – requesting many mice aged 24–30 months is logistically difficult and likely to incur high attrition.
- **Unspecified cumulative burden** – no clear limits on how many imaging and intravital sessions each animal would undergo.

## Ethical/Scientific Intent
- The scientific goal is a systems‑level understanding of immune‑vascular aging in vivo, which is hard to obtain without such a complex design.

## Required Conceptual Fixes
- **Realistic design**: down‑scale animal numbers, set realistic age windows, and model expected attrition explicitly.
- **Per‑animal burden caps**: specify maximum numbers of imaging/intravital sessions per mouse and justify them in terms of data gained.
- **Clarify sex and strain use**: correct GM labels, explain sex choices per strain, and show how the design supports reduction and refinement.
