# Case IL‑034‑12‑2000 (90034) – Biomimetic Rune-particles in Pregnant Breast‑Cancer Model (ד״ר ברק)

## Summary
- Mouse model combining pregnancy and breast cancer to assess therapeutic effects of biomimetic rune-particles.
- Includes tumor induction in pregnant dams, IVIS imaging, and euthanasia of dams and pups at defined timepoints.

## Main Reasons for Rejection
- **Euthanasia method alignment (dams)** – text suggests using CO₂ plus cervical dislocation under anesthesia, but the formal euthanasia field lists only cervical dislocation; committee prefers both methods to be explicitly required.
- **Euthanasia method for fetuses/pups** – for fetuses at the described ages, the protocol must explicitly state an appropriate method (decapitation, cervical dislocation, or pentobarbital injection); this was missing or only implied in highlighted text.

## Ethical/Scientific Intent
- The study aims to model a very challenging clinical situation (cancer during pregnancy) while adhering to high welfare standards for both dams and offspring.

## Required Conceptual Fixes
- **Explicit dual‑method euthanasia**: clearly require CO₂ followed by cervical dislocation for adult dams and list both methods in the euthanasia section.
- **Age‑appropriate methods for pups**: specify decapitation, cervical dislocation, or pentobarbital injection as the euthanasia method for fetuses/pups, aligned with local regulations.
- **Consistent documentation**: ensure these choices appear in all relevant fields, not only in highlighted narrative text.
