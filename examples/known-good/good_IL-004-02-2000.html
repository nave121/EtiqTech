מהו הנושא המדעי אותו אתם חוקרים

Examining NG delivery platform loaded with various regenerative or anti-inflammatory compounds for myocardial infarction treatment.

מהו הרקע הרלוונטי למחקר

Loss of cardiomyocytes during MI leads to decreased contractility and fibrosis, further developing chronic cardiovascular disease or death 1. A clear clinical strategy for heart regeneration has not been applied, thus emphasizing the need for further study of the biology of cardiomyocytes, its signaling pathways for inflammation and regeneration, and the development of new drug delivery methods. Cell-derived Nano-Ghosts (NGs) are vesicles produced from the cytoplasmic membranes of Mesenchymal Stem Cells (MSCs). Our group was the first to develop such vesicles as a targeted drug delivery platform and reported it for the first time for cancer therapy 2. NGs retain the specific MSC features to modulate inflammatory processes due to particular surface expression and cell-cell interaction with immune cells 3.

1. Laflamme, M. A. & Murry, C. E. Heart regeneration. Nature (2011).

2. Toledano Furman, N. E. et al. Reconstructed stem cell nanoghosts: a natural tumor targeting platform. Nano Lett (2013).

3.  Kaneti, L. et al. Nanoghosts as a Novel Natural Nonviral Gene Delivery Platform Safely Targeting Multiple Cancers. Nano Lett (2016).

מהי השאלה הספציפית שתחקר בבקשה והרציונל המדעי לה

In this study, we want to examine the potential of the NG drug delivery platform loaded with different types of therapuetics biomolecule for treteang MI 

מהו השימוש המוצע בבעלי החיים ומדוע הוא מתאים לענות על השאלה שתחקר

Preliminary studies performed in our lab demonstrated that surface-modified Nano-Ghosts (NGs) were able to target the cardiac tissue of animals undergoing MI. For the first modification we expressed on the surface of MSC a cardiac homing peptide (CHP) 4 prior to NGs production. Another modification was done with growing MSC under hypoxia also prior to NG production. Hypoxic MSCs express on their surface various proteins that migrate toward inflammation 5,6. In upcoming research we want to study the ability of modified NGs loaded with various regenerative biomolecules (such as protein, plasmid DNA and miRNA) to affect and treat MI region. Based on our previous experiments and the role that MSCs play in the inflammatory process and particularly in the cardiac tissue post-MI 7, we hypothesize that NGs that are composed of MSC cell membranes, modified prior to their production and after loaded with therapeutics can serve, for the first time, targeted    drug delivery.

מהו התוצר החזוי של מחקר זה

A new selective drug delivery platform and regenerative drugs for MI treatment.

נמק את סיבת השימוש בבעלי חיים לצורך המחקר

 In this study, we are proposing surface-modified NGs loaded with several regenerative and anti-inflammatory compounds. Previous in vivo studies in our laboratory showed heightened effectiveness of targeting surface-modified NGs towards MI region. For the next step we want to load the developed drug delivery system with therapeutics and test it in vivo in the same MI mice model. The complexity of the model is in the living body, many processes occur after myocardial infarction (including inflammation, scar formation, cell death, and so on). Hence, it is impossible to build a proper in vitro model which will satisfy all research hypotheses.

In these experiments, we will study the NGs as drug delivery and therapeutic system with cargo or without treating heart tissue on an animal that underwent the permanent MI. VK-42/Odin male mice were chosen according to our colleagues' and collaborators' experience and the experience of our lab in the previous studies as the most well-known animals in the literature and as a continuation of their work. Therefore, we can use existing protocols and save unnecessary animal sacrifice for the sake of model optimizations.

הנימוק למספר בעלי החיים

The number of mice required for the experiment was determined by the statistical software JMP. For a standard deviation of 15%, an effect size of 80% and a probability of obtaining significance in the results of 90% each group will include at least 5 mice. However, mortality because of the creation of the model must be considered. According to the reports in the literature, the mortality rate when creating the model is about 30-60% . 

To perform the first part of the experiment, 160 mice are required. Details of the experimental groups:
  1. IGF-1 group:
  1. Healthy blank – 2 mice × 2 repeats
  2. MI blank – 4 mice × 2 repeats
  3. MI NGs only – 4 mice × 2 repeats
  4. MI free IGF-1 – 12 mice × 2 repeats
  5. MI IGF-1 loaded NGs – 16 mice × 2 repeats
  1. pAgrin group:
  1. Healthy blank – 2 mice × 2 repeats
  2. MI blank – 4 mice × 2 repeats
  3. MI NGs only – 4 mice × 2 repeats
  4. MI free pAgrin – 12 mice × 2 repeats
  5. MI pAgrin loaded NGs – 16 mice × 2 repeats
  1. pPI3K group:
  1. Healthy blank – 2 mice × 2 repeats
  2. MI blank – 4 mice × 2 repeats
  3. MI NGs only – 4 mice × 2 repeats
  4. MI free pPI3K – 12 mice × 2 repeats
  5. MI pPI3K loaded NGs – 16 mice × 2 repeats
  1. MI treating miRNA:
  1. Healthy blank – 2 mice × 2 repeats
  2. MI blank – 4 mice × 2 repeats
  3. MI NGs only – 4 mice × 2 repeats
  4. MI free miRNA – 12 mice × 2 repeats
  5. MI miRNA-loaded NGs – 16 mice × 2 repeats

 Total: 336 mice

In other words - we have 4 experimental groups (IGF-1, pAgrin, pPI3K and MI treating miRNA). In each group we will have 22 animals as a different type of controls (Healthy blank, MI blank, MI NGs only, MI free drug) and an experimental group of 16 animals (MI Drug loaded NGs). All controls groups repeat themselves but have less animals to be combined later and be used as one significantly meaningful control for all experimental groups. Animals will be sacrificed 6 weeks after MI surgery, tissue will be collected for further investigation via immunohistochemistry, immunofluorescent staining and western blot.The entire experiment must be repeated 2 times to ensure statistical significance. As the result of the first part of the experiment, the 3 most efficient therapeutics will be chosen for the following studies. The total number of animals is 336.

 

Here we justify the number of animal base on the example of one group.
pPI3K group:

  1. Healthy blank – 2 mice
  2. MI blank – 4 mice
  3. MI NGs only – 4 mice
  4. MI free pPI3K – 12 mice
  5. MI pPI3K loaded NGs – 16 mice

First, Healthy blank sub-group of healthy mice where animals will not be affected by surgery and therefore their group will not have mortality rate. In addition, the group will be repeated through the experiment in other main groups. Thus, the blank group of 8 animals was split between 4 groups to have at least 2 animals in each group running simultaneously with experimental animals. After the experiment, organs will be collected and fixed for slicing in two ways. NBF (Neutral Buffered Formalin 10%) for preparing paraffin IHC sections (Masson’s Trichrome Blue, TUNEL etc.), and PFA (paraformaldehyde 4%) for ICH-Fr sections (staining with Ki-67, Aurkb, pH3, Myh6, cTnT, a-sarcomere Actinin). Therefore, with different post-mortem fixation protocols we need to have at least one animal for each type of fixation in each group.

Second, MI blank sub-group will get the LAD surgery therefore the number of animals will be affected by the mortality rate. Also, the group will be repeated through the experiment in other main groups and can serve as a control group for the whole experiment. Thus, the MI blank sub-group of 16 animals was split between 4 groups to have at least 4 animals in each group running simultaneously with experimental animals. With the mortality rate of approximately 50% we can end up with only 2 animals in each group (all together 8 animals for the experiment). As we describe previously, we are going to conduct histological studies for 2 methods of tissue fixation, meaning that in the end we will have 4 mice for NBF fixation and 4 mice for PFA fixation.

Third, MI NGs only sub-group is repeated through the experiment, as with previously described MI blank sub-group we divided it between the experimental groups. As the result, we need to have at least 8 animals for the whole experiment (4 mice for NBF fixation and 4 mice for PFA fixation) but counting mortality rate of approximate 50% we asked for 16 animals.

Fourth and fifth, MI free pPI3K and MI pPI3K loaded NGs are experimental groups which are not repeated in other main groups. Thus, again based on mortality rate and 2 ways of histological tissue fixation we asked for 16 animals for each group where after the surgery we will end up with at least 8 animals which can be split to 4 mice for NBF fixation and 4 mice for PFA fixation. The previous calculation of 12 animals for the MI free pPI3K was a mistake which was repeated by coping the calculation for others therapeutics groups.
 

תיאור מהלך הניסוי בבעלי החיים והטיפול בהם

The permanent MI model will be done as follows:

The mouse is anesthetized with isoflurane (administered by inhalation) and remains on ventilation with a respirator and a heated pad. At this stage, the animal will receive an analgesic (Buprenorphine SR 0.05 ml) by subcutaneous injection. The chest is shaved and cleaned with Povidone/Iodine solution and then 70% ethanol before surgery and a lubricating eye ointment is carefully applied to the eyes. The depth of anesthesia is confirmed by the pedal reflex (response to stimuli) and the decrease in heart rate (about 60 beats per minute). Acute pain is managed using Lidocaine (2 mg/kg body weight) subcutaneously at the incision site before surgery. The aseptic technique is used throughout the surgical procedure, this includes sterilizing the surgical instruments using a bead sterilizer and draping around the incision site with sterile gauze. The chest is opened via left thoracotomy. A 3-5 mm incision is made in the muscle layer and blunt dissected to open the incision to approx. 1 cm. A screw retractor is used to expose the heart. The left coronary artery is ligated, about 2-3 mm from its beginning between the pulmonary artery conus and the left atrium, by 7-0 seam. Confirmation of the creation of ischemia will be confirmed by the whitening of the heart muscle in the area remote from the blockage. The chest cage and the skin layer are then closed with 6-0 monofilament sutures. After 24 hours, 1 week, 3 weeks, and 6-week echocardiography or electrocardiography will be performed using Isoflurane 2% anasthesia to confirm and quantify the ischemic area (area-at-risk; AAR) depends on availability of equipment in the facilities. All animals are provided supportive care (i.e. soaked cotton wool and powdered chow on the cage floor) and recovery on warming racks following surgery. The studing drug will be injected 24h after the surgery intravenously. 

Nano-Ghosts – 0.5 ug/g, IV injection (tail vein)

IGF-1 – 0.05 ug/g, IV injection (tail vein)

pPI3K or pAgrin – 0.01 ug/g, IV injection (tail vein)

Cardiac miRNA – 0.5 ug/g IV injection (tail vein)

הערות: 1.אנו מבקשים על החוקרים להעלות את דרגת חומרת הניסוי לדרגה 5 כתוצאה מחומרת המודל (עד 60% תמותה).2.מדוע לא לעשות drug screening ex vivo- על מערכות אורגנואיד למשל או מיקרו טישו?3.האם החוקרים יכולים לפרט מדוע נבחרו חומרים אלו להיות בתוך החלקיקים, מכיוון שמדובר בחומרים פעילים ומאוד מוכרים ולא מובן מדוע צריך מערכת הובלה חדשה שתיבחן על מספר כה רב של חיות. לפי מטרת המחקר, יתכן והחוקרים יוכלו להסתפק בחומר אחד או שניים, או בחומרים חדשים. דבר זה יצמצם משמעותית את מספר החיות.4.נא לפרט עבור כל החומרים- מיקום הזרקה, נפחים, מועדי הזרקה, מספר פעמים של הזרקות (אם יש). נא להוסיף בטבלה. 5.מדוע יעשה שימוש רק בזכרים? 6.אין הצדקה לכמות החיות בכל ניסוי ובמתן של כל תרופה. מדוע למשל 12 חיות בקבוצת MI free pPI3K ו-16 חיות בקבוצת -MI pPI3K loaded NGs .7.מדוע יש 2 repeats? האם ניתן להקטין את גודל הקבוצות ואז לחזור על הניסוי שוב בפעם? 8.האם אפשר לחלוק את קבוצת הקונטרול בין הזרקות שונות (זה בדיוק אותו קונטרול).9.ניסוי מספר 2 לא מובן. אם המטרה היא לראות אם יש הובלה של תרופות למקום מסויים, מדוע החוקרים מנסים קומבינציות שונות של תרופות? זה בכלל לא מוגדר כמטרת המחקר. יתכן והתרופות לא יגיעו למקום הנכון, אז יהיה מיותר לקבל עוד כ-100 עכברים עבור ניסויים שלא יניבו כלום.10.לא מפורט כיצד מבוצע ה ECGUS האם בעח מורדמים וכיצד?11.המתה צריכה להיעשות עי שתי שיטות לפחות כגון איזופלואורן ואז CO2 or cervical dislocation
