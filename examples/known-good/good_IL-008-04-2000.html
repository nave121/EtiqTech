מהו הנושא המדעי אותו אתם חוקרים

The gut microbiome is well documented and has been shown to impact the host’s immune system and physiology. These impacts have been connected to several diseases including inflammatory disease. The mechanisms in which the gut microbiome modulates the immune system are still ambiguous and yet to be fully understood. One mechanism is communication via bacterial derived compounds, yet only a handful of molecules have been discovered.

The gut microbiota, produce and secrete metabolites as biproducts of their mechanism, at least some of which are expected to be volatile organic compounds (VOCs) that are often ignored when it comes to gut microbe-host immune communication. Here we examine the effect of bacterial derived VOC, Mead-ester compound, on the response to gut inflammation in a chemically induced colitis model.

מהו הרקע הרלוונטי למחקר

We’ve recently profiled VOC profiles of gut bacterial species using GC-MS. We identified one VOC, Mead-ester compound, derived from Bacteroides uniformis, a human gut-derived bacteria, with the potential to obtain immunomodulatory effect.

Mead-ester compound is a known chemical used in preparation of commercial orange juice and is FDA approved:

https://www.hfpappexternal.fda.gov/scripts/fdcc/index.cfm?set=FoodSubstances&id=ETHYLBUTYRATE

For the colitis model, we plan to use dextran sulfate sodium (DSS) at 3% in drinking water. A well-known colitis model, widely used in research to test the inflammatory response in the gut.

מהי השאלה הספציפית שתחקר בבקשה והרציונל המדעי לה

Here we want to establish that gut bacterial derived VOCs have the potential to be bioactive, specifically, immunomodulatory. As a proof of concept, we aim to administer Mead-ester compound via drinking water or oral gavage to ameliorate DSS induced gut inflammation. Therefore, we will be able to show that Mead-ester compound has anti-inflammatory impact, and that bacterial VOCs in general have the capability to affect the mammalian host.

מהו השימוש המוצע בבעלי החיים ומדוע הוא מתאים לענות על השאלה שתחקר

We propose to study the effect that Mead-ester compound administration would have induce in the context of chemically induced colitis. Mead-ester compound would be administered in drinking water or oral gavage, throughout the experiment. While DSS will be administered in drinking water to induce colitis.

These experimental set-ups are well documented and widely used in the literature to establish anti-inflammatory effect of a specific agent. In our case, we will test Mead-ester compound’s impact.

מהו התוצר החזוי של מחקר זה

The potential results of the research could be understanding the mechanisms in which gut microbiota can affect the onset and development of colitis and inflammatory bowel disease (IBD) and translating this knowledge into developing microbiota-based interventions to modulate IBD severity.

Moreover, our results, would provide a proof of concept that gut bacterial VOCs can affect the mammalian host. Opening the doors for a wide library of previously ignored bacterial molecules to be examined for their bioactivity.

נמק את סיבת השימוש בבעלי חיים לצורך המחקר

To establish a clear anti-inflammatory effect, we must present data showing that this effect has both molecular level proof and clinical improvement in the presence of inflammation. As we will present in the “previous experiments” section, we have tested Mead-ester compound in vitro and have shown that it has anti-inflammatory effects. Yet, the only way we can establish a clear, undisputable, connection would require in vivo set up.

Amelioration of gut inflammation, and anti-inflammatory effect in general ought to be tested in a systematic manner to produce reliable results. Furthermore, inflammatory response is a complex and systematic response that can’t be represented in vitro.
