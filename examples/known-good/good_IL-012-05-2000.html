מהו הנושא המדעי אותו אתם חוקרים

The purpose of this proposal is to explore how immune cells influence the ECM of pancreatic cancer and to evaluate the effects on tumor growth and metastasis.

מהו הרקע הרלוונטי למחקר

Pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC) presents a fatal clinical challenge characterized by a poor survival rate, primarily due to the lack of early diagnosis and limited therapeutic efficacy. Although gemcitabine in combination with combination of other chemotherapies is the first-line standard of care treatment, the clinical median survival is still under 1 year. While immunotherapy with immune checkpoint inhibitors (ICIs) has proven success in several cancers, it has not yet demonstrate significant benefits for PDAC patients. The tumor microenvironment (TME) in PDAC is considered to be with immunosuppressive characteristics, a major factor that might contribute to immunotherapy resistance. This includes tumor-infiltrating immune-suppressive cells, stromal cells, and the extracellular matrix (ECM).

The ECM is an acellular protein scaffold within tumors, providing structural support that typically promotes cancer cell proliferation, local invasion, and metastasis. In our previous studies, we have demonstrated the involvement of immune cells in supporting tumor growth both in untreated and treated tumors. These effects are linked to programing of immune cells into tumor supporting stroma, further contributing to their pro-tumorigenic activities. Notably, our recent results demonstrated that these effects are associated with ECM remodeling mediated by specific immune cells, particularly at pre-metastatic sites. While most studies in the field emphasize the role of cancer-associated fibroblasts (CAFs) in tumor ECM remodeling, our recent findings propose that immune cells can actively reshape the ECM to facilitate tumor growth and metastasis. Therefore, the involvement of immune cells-induced ECM remodeling following immunotherapy worth a comprehensive study as a better understanding of the mechanisms involved in the immune evasion of pancreatic cells, with particular attention on the role of immune cells-ECM crosstalk might provide the basis for improving immunotherapy by identifying new targets and predictive biomarkers. Exploring combination strategies involving immunotherapy and agents tailored to target these TME characteristics has emerged as a prominent area of research in pancreatic cancer.

מהי השאלה הספציפית שתחקר בבקשה והרציונל המדעי לה

In this research, we will study PDAC. Our objective in this study is to elucidate interaction of immune cells and the ECM at the PDAC TME. In this study, we will ask how immune cells support ECM remodeling. We aim to elucidate what the role of both myeloid and lymphoid immune cells is in reshaping PDAC ECM that can explain cancer aggressiveness, as well as resistance to immunotherapy.

מהו התוצר החזוי של מחקר זה

Deciphering the mechanisms by which specific immune cells contribute to PDAC-ECM remodeling is crucial to understand its growth and metastasis. The research outcome will demonstrate how immune system modulation influences the regulatory function of the ECM in PDAC tumors and therefore may lead to developing treatments associated with pathological tumor-ECM conditions. Moreover, the suggested research will potentially reveal new ECM- immune cells related discoveries regarding resistance of PDAC towards immunotherapy therefore pointing upon new targets to overcome such resistance.

The study will involve immune-competent mice, as mice are the ultimate use for studying cancer, as they are low vertebrates that can mimic human pathological conditions. In this study, we will focus on studying the ECM of pancreatic tumors at the primary tumor as well as metastatic sites. As such, VK-42/Odin mice were chosen to test the effect of immune cells on tumor ECM in unperturbed tumors or following immunotherapy. The experiments will be using live imaging with ultrasound and IVIS to monitor tumor growth and metastasis. This will allow the use of a smaller number of mice, since we will not have to sacrifice the mice at each time point that will be tested.

תיאור מהלך הניסוי בבעלי החיים והטיפול בהם

 

General description - in this experiment, we will identify immune cells and molecular factors that are found and expressed in the PDAC tumor microenvironment (TME), that play a role in tumor ECM remodulation and affect tumor growth and metastasis. This will be examined within tumors as well as systematically in the peripheral blood, and immune organs such as the spleen. In addition, metastatic sites (e.g., liver) will be examined as well. The above-mentioned will be examined in vivo by ultrasound (US) and IVIS imaging for tumor growth and spread, as well as postmortem for their ECM by means of histological and biochemical tests, and the composition of immune cells by means such as single-cell RNA-seq and flow cytometry analyses. Novel factors will be identified in the plasma and in the conditioned medium of tumor cells using mass spectrometry and cytokine array. Of note, we will also study how treatment with anti-Gleipnir-1 immunotherapy and combination with gemcitabine chemotherapy affects the ECM at the TME and what immune cells are involved in such processes.

Experimental procedures:

Primary tumor orthotopic implantation:

For orthotopic tumor, we will use operation as follows: Subconfluent Panc02 murine pancreatic tumor cells grown in fresh medium 24 h before injection. Cells then will be resuspended at a concentration of 5x10^5 cells in 15 ul of Matrigel. VK-42/Odin mice will be receiving analgesic (Long-term Buprenorphine 3.25mg/kg body weight - SC). Then mice will be operated under anesthesia, either with isoflurane (induction with 4% per litter, and maintenance in a dose of 2-3% isoflurane per litter, or with ketamine (65 mg/kg) and xylazine (6.5 mg/kg) i.p. injection (100ul). The abdomen then will be applied with a disinfection solution. A small cut is made in the skin at the abdominal wall, and abdominal cavity is exposed at the upper side, following incision of 0.5 cm. The pancreas is then exposed, and the tumor cells will be injected into the head of the pancreas using a Hamilton glass syringe and 30-gauge needle. The mouse abdominal wall will be then closed using two to three interrupted stitches (using poly glycolic acid suture). Mice are then allowed to recover from anesthesia, and post-operative analgesics with Buprenorphine will be given every 3 days. The growth of primary tumors implanted orthotopically into the pancreas will be monitored once a week by IVIS (we have these cells expressing luciferase) and ultrasound (US), both are found in our SPF unit. Panc02 tumors form metastasis thus the IVIS we will be used to monitor metastasis spread as well. Metastases appear usually within 3 weeks following tumor inoculation, where the main metastatic sites of these tumors is liver, stomach and intestine. The well-being of the mice will be monitored carefully for any signs of body weight loss, fatigue and general condition worsening, as well as behavioral change reflecting worsening in the general condition of mice because of metastasis. Examples of scoring and follow-up tables are also given in the appendix. At endpoint (tumor size of 60mm^3, as determined by US, or no later than 21 days), blood will be drawn from mice by facial vein bleeding (or cardiac puncture) under anesthesia (see elaborated procedure below under General procedures) and subsequently will be sacrificed by cervical dislocation. Thereafter tumors, as well as organs (liver, stomach, and intestine), will be collected for histopathology analysis and CyTOF experiment to identify immune cells at the TMA or metastatic sites. An example of tumor growth US and curve is presented in the appendix.

Specific description of the experiments:

A) Immune cell composition analysis and ECM analysis in the pancreatic cancer TME.

(i) A CyTOF scan analysis for the immune cell composition in the TME, and tumor growth and metastasis spread comparison in mice bearing pancreatic tumors. Panc02 pancreatic cancer cells will be implanted orthotopically as described above in VK-42/Odin mice. Tumor growth will be monitored weekly by IVIS and US. After 21 days, blood will be drawn (by facial bleeding or cardiac puncture – see below under General Procedures), the pancreas which include also the tumor, will be removed and will be processed as single-cell suspension for CyTOF scan analysis of the composition of immune cells at the TME. In addition, liver, stomach and intestine will be removed and will be analyzed for histopathology. Using the CyTOF scan, we will get the overall immunogenic representation of the pancreatic cancer TME, because the CyTOF enables the use of over 30 markers for the detection of multipleimmune cells. A group of non-tumor bearing mice will serve as control (7 mice in this group is sufficient). [2 groups - 22 mice]. The experiment will be performed twice.

(ii) In a subsequent experiment, mice will be treated identically as in (i), but at endpoint, the pancreas along with the tumors will be removed and processed as single-cell suspension for standard flow cytometry validation of selected immune cells of interest discovered by the CyTOF scan. In addition, tumors (and organs) will be sectioned for histopathology analyses, by hematoxyline and eosin (H&E) staining, as well as Sirius red staining (for ECM texture). A group of non-tumor bearing mice will serve as control (7 mice in this group is sufficient) [2 groups – 22 mice]. The experiment will be performed twice.

Of note, humane endpoint will be determined when tumors reach the size of 60mm^3 monitored by US (or 21 days after tumor inoculation). Experiment is stopped when tumor size reaches this point or no later than 21 days post neoplasm seeding, in the case tumors did not get to the above-mentioned size. Further considerations are depicted under General considerations taken to ensure welfare of mice and humane endpoint.

For this set of experiments (i+ii) 15 mice per group are required. For each sub section a group of non-tumor bearing mice will be served as control (in non-tumor bearing mice n=7 is sufficient) The experiments will be repeated twice.

Therefore, in this section we will use 88 mice.

B) scRNAseq analysis of the pancreatic cancer TME. In this experiment, we will use single cell RNA sequencing (scRNAseq), to focus deeper on small sub-populations of immune cells at the TME of pancreatic tumors. Here as well we will orthotopically implant these Panc02 luciferase cells as described above. We will monitor the tumor growth with IVIS once twice a week. Mice welfare considerations and humane endpoint are similar to the description above in A. At endpoint (after 21 days or when tumors reach the size of 60mm^3) mice will be sacrificed, and tumors will be removed, and tumor will be extracted and processed as single-cell suspension for scRNAseq analysis. To do this, 15 mice are required for primary tumor growth. Using advanced bioinformatic methodologies this experiment will not only point out small sub-populations of immune cells at the TME but also will provide molecular pathways and genes that are involved in PDAC growth and aggressiveness. 

A group of non-tumor bearing mice will be served as control. [2 groups - 22 mice]. The experiments will be conducted twice. 15 mice per group are required, 7 for non-tumor mice; therefore, in this section we will use 44 mice.

C) The evaluation of molecular factors expressed in the TME of pancreatic tumors.

In this experiment, we will orthotopically implant pancreatic tumors as described above. Here, at endpoint, tumors will be removed, blood will be drawn, and plasma will be produced. Thereafter (i) the removed pancreas with tumors will be cultured in vitro for 24-48 hours, and then conditioned media (CM) will be collected and stored in -80C until analysis. (ii) Blood will be processed as plasma and the plasma samples will be stored frozen at -80C until analysis. Then the CM and plasma samples will be analyzed for different cytokines and growth factors using cytokine arrays (R&D). This experiment will bridge between the results obtained in the former two experiments (CyTOF and scRNAseq) and will further validate factors that are involved in PDAC tumor progression and aggression.

A group of non-tumor bearing mice will be served as control. [2 groups - 22 mice].

The experiments will be conducted twice. 15 mice per group are required in tumor-bearing mice; 7 mice per group in non-tumor-bearing mice therefore, therefore in this section we will use 44 mice.

D) Study the efficacy of immunotherapy and chemotherapy and their combination in pancreatic tumors.

(i) The pancreatic tumor ECM is desmoplastic and thus can affect therapy efficacy. Therefore, we will test the effects of chemotherapy, immunotherapy and their combinations on tumor growth and tis ECM. For this, we will implant Panc02 and tumors and tumor growth will be monitored by as described above. After 7 days, treatment with anti-Gleipnir-1 immunotherapy or control IgG (Bio X Cell), gemcitabine (MCE), or the combination of gemcitabine and anti-Gleipnir-1 will be initiated.

1) Anti-Gleipnir-1 (or control IgG) will be given intraperitoneal (i.p.) injections of 100ug/100ul in PBS (5mg/kg), one shot every 3 days, for 3 shots total. Rat IgG2a will be use as isotype control and will be injected in a similar way and dose as anti-Gleipnir-1. Of note, the dosage of anti-Gleipnir-1 and isotype control is in agreement with guidelines applied by Charles River (https://www.criver.com/resources/syngeneic-model-data).

2) Gemcitabine will be given once a week at a maximum tolerated dose (MTD) of 500 mg/kg (10mg/200ul per mouse; i.p., for 2 shots total).

3) Combination therapy will include the administration of 100ug/100ul anti-Gleipnir-1, one shot every 3 days and 10mg/200ul gemcitabine once a week (total of 3 shots of anti-Gleipnir-1 and 2 shots of gemcitabine). A group of tumor bearing mice that will not receive treatment will serve as control. We should note that both the treatments with anti-Gleipnir-1 (and IgG isotype control), and gemcitabine are not inducing any toxicities nor induce discomfort to the mice, and are well practiced in our laboratory.

Tumor growth will be there further assessed with IVIS and US as above. Mice welfare considerations and humane endpoint are similar to the description above in A. 21 days post tumor inoculation, mice will be scarified, blood will be drawn (by facial bleeding or cardiac puncture – see below under General Procedures) and tumors will be removed for flow cytometry analysis of immune cells at the TME and histopathology for ECM structure and components. As above, histology analyses will include both H&E and Sirius red staining and staining with antibodies for specific ECM factors (e.g., collagens, elastin, fibronectin and laminin). Plasma will be obtained from the blood and will be applied on cytokine arrays to determine the protein profile associated with resistance to immunotherapy. Elected cytokines that their expression levels found to be elevated in Panc02 tumors will be validated by ELISA. [4 groups: IgG, anti-Gleipnir-1, gemcitabine , anti-Gleipnir-1 + gemcitabine) - 60 mice]. The experiments will be done twice.

(ii) The evaluation of validated cytokines on therapy resistance.

Here we will examine whether Panc02-derived plasma can induce therapy resistance and whether the cytokines found to be elevated upon therapy are indeed contributing to therapy resistance. The following set of experiment will be thus conducted: VK-42/Odin mice will be orthotopically implanted with Panc02 as described above. Mice will be then treated with anti-Gleipnir-1 as described above in (i). One day after the third treatment blood will be drawn and the mice will be sacrificed. Plasma will be then produced (5 plasma donors mice per experimental group (15 mice) is sufficient, see below). Thereafter, new VK-42/Odin mice will be orthotopically implanted with Panc02. After 1-week, treatments with i.p. injections of plasma (100ul, once a week, 2 shots in total) as following: Panc02 bearing mice will receive plasma derived from Panc2 plasma donor mice, will receive plasma derived donor mice.  Plasma groups will include non-treated plasma, and plasma group that was depleted in vitro to a certain validated cytokine suspected to be account or involved in

- General procedures:

The following procedures will be applied on some of the mice, in some experiments, based on the necessity of such procedure for each tumor model or experimental plan described above.

1. Facial vein bleeding:

Each mouse is anesthetized using open-drop exposure to isoflurane inhalant anesthesia for induction using a concentration of 2-3%. The mouse is held by the back of the neck, and the loose skin of the head is tightened with the thumb and middle finger and the mouse is placed on the wire bars of the cage. A 4mm blood lancet is used for the bleeding procedure. The tip of the lancet is pointed right over the mouse chick at the hairless freckle on the side of the jaw, and a short and sharp thrust is then applied. As soon as the chick is punctured, blood is dropped into a pre-containing heparin small blood collecting tube. When the allowable amount of blood is collected, a piece of gauze on the chick is used to prevent further bleeding, and the mouse is returned to its cage.

2. Cardiac puncture:

Mice are being maintained with anesthesia using isoflurane at a concentration of 2-3% per liter. When the mouse is asleep, the procedure is carried out using a 1 ml syringe and a 22G needle, by laying the animal on the back and pushing the syringe vertically through sternum. 0.5 cc of air is withdrawn to create vacuum in the syringe. When blood appears in the syringe is held steady and the plunger is pulled back gently to obtain the maximum amount of blood available. Post procedure, the animal euthanized by cerebral dislocation.

3. Procedures for live imaging:

In all the imaging procedures mice will be anesthetized with isoflurane by an induction in a concentration of 4% per litter, and then maintenance will be performed in a dose of 2-3% isoflurane per litter. IVIS imaging will be performed once a week. US imaging will be performed once a week as well, but not at the same day of IVIS in order to let the mice recover well from anesthesia.

Of note, all the experimental procedures described above are well practice in our lab.

פירוט המעקב הדרוש @@@@@@@The research will be conducted mostly at pain level 4 but in experiments dealing with metastases, pain level will be designated as 5. Therefore, we chose to designate the study as pain level 5 even though not all experiments will be conducted at this pain level. To adhere with this setup, animals will be under tight supervision and will be monitored twice a week, for general sighns and weight, and once a week by imaging.The following parameters will be monitored each time:1)Tumor size measured by US, and metastasis spread measured by IVIS.2)Body weight - no more than 20% body weight loss will be allowed.3)Self-ability of nourishing and drinking.4)Mouse vitality, activity and general well-being.All of the above will be documented in follow-up tables (see appendix).
תנאים כלליים להפסקת השתתפות חיה בניסוי 1) Humane endpoint is when tumors reach the size of 60mm^3 as measured by US. Experiment is stopped when tumor size reach this size or no later than 21 days post neoplasm seeding, in case tumors did not get this size. 2) Mice weight, behavior, general condition and activity will be monitored daily in the first three days (post-surgery) and then twice a week during the experiment time. Mice not active, show unusual behavior, or loss of more than 20% of their body weight will be euthanatized and excluded from the study. A supportive care such as fluids and/or painkillers will be given to mice that are losing weight. BUT, in the case of loss of more than 20% of body weight, mice will be euthanatized and excluded from the study. 3) If any pathological condition appears in a mouse that is not directly related to the study, the mouse will be euthanatize. 4) When mice are not able to reach water or food or look lethargic or do not present a typical behavior, they will be euthanatized.
תנאים ספציפיים להפסקת השתתפות חיה בניסוי In addition to the above, if an animal suffers immediate and irreversible harm or distress as a result of an experimental procedure - for example, after tumor injection, treatment injection, failure to recover from anesthesia - the animal is immediately humanely euthanized under anesthesia with isoflurane and immediate cervical dislocation
