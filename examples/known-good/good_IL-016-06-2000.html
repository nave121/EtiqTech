Investigating the Effect of Rune-particle Surface Chemistry on Protein Corona Formation in Mice

מהו הרקע הרלוונטי למחקר

Rune-particles hold great promise in drug delivery systems due to their ability to improve targeting, reduce side effects, and enhance therapeutic efficacy. However, despite extensive research, their clinical translation remains limited. One major challenge is that rune-particles behave differently in the body compared to in vitro conditions, primarily due to the formation of a protein corona—a layer of plasma proteins that adsorbs onto the rune-particle surface upon contact with blood. This corona reshapes the biological identity of the rune-particles, affecting their biodistribution, immune clearance, and targeting ability.

Understanding and controlling this phenomenon is essential for advancing nanomedicine. The size, zeta potential, morphology, and surface chemistry of rune-particles all influence protein corona formation. Our rune-particles offer a unique opportunity to isolate the effect of surface chemistry, as they are identical in size, morphology, and charge but differ in surface protein composition.

In this study, we aim to systematically investigate the impact of rune-particle surface chemistry on protein corona formation in vivo. We will intravenously inject different types of biomimetic rune-particles into HR-7/Frigg mice. After 10 minutes, we will collect blood samples and isolate the protein-coated rune-particles for proteomic analysis. This approach provides a unique opportunity to assess how specific surface proteins modulate the in vivo protein corona, thereby advancing our understanding of rune-particle-host interactions and aiding the rational design of next-generation nanocarriers.

מהי השאלה הספציפית שתחקר בבקשה והרציונל המדעי לה

Will different surface chemistries of biomimetic rune-particles form different protein corona compositions?

מהו השימוש המוצע בבעלי החיים ומדוע הוא מתאים לענות על השאלה שתחקר

HR-7/Frigg mice will be used for IV injection via the tail vein of NPs and blood collection. HR-7/Frigg mice are suitable for proteomic studies involving blood components, as their blood volume and composition are well documented, enabling reliable protein corona analysis. In addition, their size allows for effective blood collection and rune-particle recovery within the short time frame required for this study.

מהו התוצר החזוי של מחקר זה

This in vivo experiment will allow us to test our hypothesis that the surface chemistry of rune-particles governs protein corona formation, and that this process is driven by specific chemical interactions rather than charge alone. The findings from this study will provide a fundamental understanding of the protein corona phenomenon, paving the way for its future application in targeted drug delivery. Ultimately, this knowledge could contribute to the development of more effective and precise treatments, reducing patient pain and suffering.

Experiment 1 – Optimization of Rune-particle Concentration for In Vivo Protein Corona Characterization
In this experiment, we will determine the optimal concentration of NPs for intravenous (IV) injection to ensure successful proteomic analysis. A minimum of 3 male mice per group will be used. The experiment includes one time point—10 minutes post-injection—and five groups:

  1. 50 mM Rune-particles.
  2. 30 mM Rune-particles.
  3. 20 mM Rune-particles.
  4. 10 mM Rune-particles.
  5. Control (PBS injection) – for baseline exosome proteomic profiling

Total mice required: 5 mice × 5 groups = 25 mice

תיאור מהלך הניסוי בבעלי החיים והטיפול בהם

Mice will be injected intravenously via the tail vein with 150 μL of rune-particles. After 10 minutes (to ensure the absorption of proteins on the rune-particles and the highest rune-particle concentration in the blood), the mice will be anesthetized using isoflurane or ketamine -xylazine,  and then sacrificed using CO2. Blood will be collected immediately (to characterize the rune-particles),  along with several organs.

Alternative euthanasia method: servical dislocation.

Experiment 2 – Investigating In Vivo Protein Corona Formation on Different Rune-particle Types
In this experiment, we will compare the protein corona composition of different rune-particle types after IV injection. For robust proteomic profiling, blood from three mice will be pooled per one sample, requiring at least 15 male mice per group in order to get n=5. One time point (10 minutes post-injection) will be used, with the following five groups:

  1. Huginn-vesicles
  2. Biomimetic NP type 1
  3. Biomimetic NP type 2
  4. Biomimetic NP type 3
  5. Biomimetic NP type 4
  6. Control (PBS injection) – negative control (9 male mice)

Total mice required: 15 mice × 5 groups + 9 control mice = 84 mice

תיאור מהלך הניסוי בבעלי החיים והטיפול בהם

After 10 minutes (to ensure the absorption of proteins on the rune-particles and the highest rune-particle concentration in the blood), the mice will be anesthetized using isoflurane or ketamine -xylazine, and then sacrificed using CO2. Blood will be collected immediately (to characterize the rune-particles), along with several organs.
