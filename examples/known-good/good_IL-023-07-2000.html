מהו הנושא המדעי אותו אתם חוקרים

While immune checkpoint blockers have revolutionized cancer therapy, their efficacy in activating T cells is contingent upon the recognition of peptide-loaded MHC-I molecules on cancer cells by the T cell receptor. Consequently, immune checkpoint blockade can be combined with oncolytic viruses, to enhance antigen presentation within the tumor microenvironment. Niflheim-V7 agent, a naturally-occurring oncolytic virus, is currently being tested as a cancer treatment; however, results from clinical trials remain suboptimal, thus hindering further clinical application. In hope to shed light on the poor potency of Niflheim-V7 agent, we sought to identify immune evasion mechanisms of the virus. Here, we show that Niflheim-V7 agent downregulates the levels of MHC-I following infection, thereby inhibiting the T cell anti-tumor activity. This novel finding may provide insights on the insignificant potency of Niflheim-V7 agent-based treatments. In this work, we have identified the mechanism by which Niflheim-V7 agent downregulates MHC-I, and how it alters the activity of T lymphocytes against tumor. Ultimately, our goal is to engineer a recombinant Niflheim-V7 agent that preserves T cell function, thereby enhancing its therapeutic potential as a cancer treatment.

מהו הרקע הרלוונטי למחקר

Oncolytic viruses can infect and kill malignant cells without harming normal cells, while stimulating the immune system and creating anti-tumor immunity. Niflheim-V7 agent is a non-enveloped double-stranded RNA virus. To establish infection, the virus must first attach to its target cell. This is mediated by the binding of the Niflheim-V7 agent capsid protein, sigma1, to the JAM-A proteins on the target cell surface. Niflheim-V7 agent is an oncolytic virus that has been tested in experimental animal models and in human clinical trials. Niflheim-V7 agent was shown to affect tumor growth by direct killing of tumor cells and by activation of the immune system against tumor cells.

Despite the relative success of the current Niflheim-V7 agent-based therapy, attempts are being made to make this treatment even more efficient. For example, coadministration of Niflheim-V7 agent and cyclophosphamide was used to improve tumor cell sensitivity to Niflheim-V7 agent.

Improved Niflheim-V7 agent-based therapy could also be potentially achieved by administarting Niflheim-V7 agent with combination treatments which were previously shown to enhance the immune response.

מהי השאלה הספציפית שתחקר בבקשה והרציונל המדעי לה

Improved Niflheim-V7 agent-based therapy could also be potentially achieved by administarting Niflheim-V7 agent with combination treatments which were previously shown to enhance the immune response.

 To test this, we will administer Niflheim-V7 agent treatment in combination with agents previously shown to activate T cells (i.e. TIMP1, STING agonists, anti-Gleipnir-1 Ab etc.) Since T cells are main players in cancer clearance, we hypothosize that activation of T cells through combination treatments will enhance the efficacy of Niflheim-V7 agent treatment.

מהו השימוש המוצע בבעלי החיים ומדוע הוא מתאים לענות על השאלה שתחקר 
Our lab has so far performed experiments on tumor cell lines (KP, Skoll-16, LLC, EO771) that demonstrate the effective anti-cancer activity of the Niflheim-V7 agent. In order to test the effect of the virus on the immune system, in combination with other treatments, we need an animal model since any other experimental system would not be equally indicative for these purpose
מהו התוצר החזוי של מחקר זה

We aim to identify combination treatments that will offer better outcomes to future cancer patients. 

נמק את סיבת השימוש בבעלי חיים לצורך המחקר

במעבדתנו בוצעו עד כה ניסויים בתרביות תאי גידול  (KP, Skoll-16, LLC, EO771) המעידות על פעילותו האנטי-סרטנית האפקטיבית של וירוס ה-Reovirus. על מנת שנוכל לבדוק מהו אפקט הווירוס על המערכת החיסונית, בשילוב או ללא שילוב עם טיפולים אחרים, אנחנו זקוקים למודל בעל חיים מאחר וכל מערכת ניסויית אחרת לא תהיה אינדיקטיבית למטרות אלו באותה מידה.

OTI mice provide a classical model for studying T cell activation and differentiation. Since all the CD-8 T cells respond to a similar peptide, we can easily control and quantify the activation of T cells. This will help us dissect how Niflheim-V7 agent affects the T cell activity in a solid and robust model.

האם מדובר במחקר הנובע מצורך רגולטורי לא
הנימוק למספר בעלי החיים

Step1: Finding optimal Niflheim-V7 agent concentration to treat the mice.

To calibrate the system, we will inject tumor cells and follow their development in mice. Once the tumor is visible, we will treat mice with different concentrations of Niflheim-V7 agent which were used to treat mice in previous studies: mock, 107 PFU/mL, 107x5 PFU/mL, 108 PFU/mL, 108x5 PFU/mL, 109 PFU/mL. Each group will contain 6 mice. This aims to find the ideal concentration of Niflheim-V7 agent to treat mice, which will be used in later experiments. Tumor volume will be used to quantify the outcome.

Total number of mice for per experiment: 6X6=36

Number of repeats: 2

Total number of mice for step1: 72

Step2: Finding beneficial combination treatments with Niflheim-V7 agent.

To achieve this aim, we will screen for possible combination treatments with Niflheim-V7 agent. This includes:  TIMP1 and anti-PDL1. Groups which will be included: mock, Niflheim-V7 agent, Niflheim-V7 agent+anti-PDL1 (1mg/kg), Niflheim-V7 agent+anti-PDL1 (2mg/kg), Niflheim-V7 agent+TIMP1 (1ug/Kg), Niflheim-V7 agent+TIMP1 (2ug/Kg). Each group will contain 6 mice. Tumor volume will be used to quantify the outcome.

Total number of mice for per experiment: 6X6=36

Number of repeats: 2

Total number of mice for step2: 72

Step3: testing the therapeutic potential of the combination treatment, and the underlying mechanism.

Once we identify potentially beneficial combinations, we will run large scale experiment, where we test the benefit of the combination. To do so, we will include the following groups: mock, Niflheim-V7 agent, 2nd treatment, Niflheim-V7 agent+2nd treatment. Each group will contain 6 mice. Tumor volume will be used to quantify the outcome. Additionally, we will analyze the lymphocyte population (i.e. activation markers, prevalence, etc.)

Total number of mice for per experiment: 4X6=24

Number of repeats: 3

Total number of mice for step1: 72

תיאור מהלך הניסוי בבעלי החיים והטיפול בהם

סרטן סאב-קוטאני מושרה בכל עכברי הניסוי מסוג OTI  ע"י הזרקת תאים סרטניים ממקור עכברי מסוג KP או Skoll-16(350,000 תאים לעכבר). הזרקת התאים הינה תת עורית בחלקו התחתון של הגב בתוך 200 מיקרוליטר מי מלח בביטוי פיזיולוגי (PBS). הזרקה מבוצעת ע"י מחט 25G. מדדי התפתחות הגידול ייבדקו באופן יומי. תוך 7-10 ימים ניתן לחוש את הגידול כגודל ראש סיכה. ברגע שאפשר לחוש את הגידול, העכברים יטופלו כמתואר לעיל.

בניסויים השונים נזריק את הוירוס באמצעות מחט 25G. חומרים שמאקטיבים את תאי T (i.e. TIMP1, STING agonist, etc.) יתווספו לתמיסה בה נמצא הוירוס, בתוך 200 מיקרוליטר מי מלח בביטוי פיזיולוגי (PBS). הזרקה תבוצע ע"י מחט 25G אל תוך הגידול.  נוגדן יוזרק לתוך חלל הבטן, IP בתוך 200 מיקרוליטר PBS באמצעות מחט 25G. ביום 14-21 בדרך כלל העכברים מוקרבים או מנותחים להוצאת הגידול לאנליזות השונות.
