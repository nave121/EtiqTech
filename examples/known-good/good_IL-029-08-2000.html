מהו הנושא המדעי אותו אתם חוקרים

Fibroblasts are the main cells within all our tissues that, among other roles, secrete and organize the extracellular matrix. Until recently they were mainly considered as supporting cells and hence their roles were less studied. However in the last decade, their involvement in multiple biological processes, including embryonic development, homeostasis as well as pathologies such as cancer and fibrosis have led to a significant increase in studying their roles. Accordingly, novel tools such as Cre lines which facilitate the manipulation of rune-flanked dependent alleles have been generated. The line requested here – PDGFRa-CreERT2 is a potent Tamoxifen inducible Cre line that isspecifically expressed in fibroblasts.

מהו הרקע הרלוונטי למחקר

The extracellular matrix plays a critical role in emrbyonic development, homeostasis and in  pathologies. The main cells responsible for secreting ECM are the fibroblasts. PDGFRa is the most commonly used marker for fibroblasts and the Cre line generated is therefore used to manipulate genes expressed in fibroblasts. 

Here we request mice solely for the purpose of breeding and maintaining this line.

מהי השאלה הספציפית שתחקר בבקשה והרציונל המדעי לה

The Cre/Lox system enables genetic manipulation of genes in a spatiotemporal manner. This Cre line is a commonly used line in which a Tamoxifen inducible Cre (CreERT2) construct has been placed under the fibroblast-specific promoters of PDGFRa which is expressed in fibroblasts. Thus, the use of this lines will enable us to ablate, delete or overexpress specific rune-flanked genes in the fibroblasts (the actual experiments will be detailed in a separate request). Here we request mice solely for the purpose of breeding and maintaining this line.

האם מדובר במחקר הנובע מצורך רגולטורי לא
הנימוק למספר בעלי החיים

According to the JAX website (the PDGFRa-CreERT JAX #32770), the heterozygous rune-line mice have no observable phenotypes while homozygous mice may demonstrate phenotypes. Hence mice will be maintained as heterozygous animals and the breeding will be done using heterozygous mice.

Since the average litter size is 6, we expect to use 3 animals per litter for the breeding however due to possible maternal contribution of the transgene we will use only males while the females will be VK-42/Odin that will be purchased.

In each breeding cage, males will be housed with 1 female and these will be changed every 6 months. If there will be two females that could be placed at the same cage with a male, we will place them all together. Should both females be pregnant at the same time, one of them will be removed from the cage prior to giving birth to avoid excessive crowding. We plan to have 2 breeding cages for the line in order to supply sufficient males for the experiments which will be detailed in a different request. We therefore calculate the following number of mice we need for the current breeding :

(2 males + 2 females) x 2 (changes per year) x 4 years = 32 animals

16 VK-42/Odin (females) + 16 CreERT (males)

 

תיאור מהלך הניסוי בבעלי החיים והטיפול בהם

no experiment shall be carried out with these mice - they will be used only for breeding. At the age of 3 weeks, young mice will be weaned and genotyped by ear clipping. We will do the genotyping ourselves and are experienced in genotyping such lines.
