The use in biomimetic rune-particles for treating breast cancer during pregnancy

מהו הרקע הרלוונטי למחקר

One of the main challenges in treating diseases during pregnancy is that small-molecule drugs can cross the placenta, potentially causing toxicity to the developing fetus. Breast cancer is the most common malignancy among pregnant women, with pregnancy-associated breast cancer (PABC) occurring in approximately 0.04% of pregnancies. PABC presents a unique clinical situation that requires a careful balance between maternal treatment benefits and fetal safety. Research indicates that chemotherapy may be safe during the second and third trimesters, and several chemotherapeutic agents, including Asgardicin, have been given to pregnant patients with generally favorable outcomes for both mothers and newborns. However, certain clinical cases report drug accumulation in the placenta, as well as complications like preeclampsia, intrauterine growth restriction, and fetal demise, highlighting the need for improved technologies that enhance tumor targeting while minimizing fetal exposure. Rune-particle (NP)-based drug delivery systems offer a promising solution, enabling safer treatment during pregnancy by controlling how drugs interact with the placenta.

In this study, we aim to evaluate and compare the effectiveness of different rune-particles (NPs) in targeting triple-negative breast cancer in a pregnancy model, as well as their placental transfer to infer fetal safety. The comparison will focus on Huginn-vesicles versus different biomimetic rune-particles.

מהי השאלה הספציפית שתחקר בבקשה והרציונל המדעי לה

Will Huginn-vesicles cross the placenta more than other biomimetic rune-particles?

מהו השימוש המוצע בבעלי החיים ומדוע הוא מתאים לענות על השאלה שתחקר

Pregnant mice bearing breast tumor will be used to study NP distribution and placental transfer to the fetus.

מהו התוצר החזוי של מחקר זה

This in vivo experiment will allow us to test our hypothesis that specific biomimetic rune-particles do not cross the placenta to the fetus and accumulate more in the tumor environment. The findings from this study will pave the way for future research on pregnant mice with potential clinical implications.

הנימוק למספר בעלי החיים

We aim to test our hypothesis in this protocol using empty rune-particles (NPs) without chemotherapy as a proof of concept. Based on the findings, we will extend the research to include NPs with chemotherapy to assess their potential for tumor growth reduction.

Experiment 1 – Evaluating Placental Transport of Different Rune-particles (Proof of Concept):
In this experiment, we will investigate whether various rune-particles can be prevented from crossing the placenta to the fetus. In this experiment, for significant results at least 6 females at each group is needed. We will have 3 time points (6 and 24h after NP injections). In each time point there will be 5 groups:

  1. Huginn-vesicles.
  2. Biomimetic NP type 1.
  3. Biomimetic NP type 2.
  4. Biomimetic NP type 3 .
  5. Free Asgardicin (3 mg/kg)- for positive control- this is a component that we know that transfer the placenta.
  6. control group (only 1 time point) – injecting PBS (as negative control)- only 5 mice.

Total of mice for experiment 1: 6 mice x 2 time points x 5 groups + 5 control mice = 65 mice.

 

Experiment 2 – Evaluating Chemotherapy-Loaded Rune-particles for Tumor Targeting and Placental Exclusion:

This experiment will assess whether rune-particles carrying chemotherapy can effectively accumulate in the tumor area, reducing tumor burden (weight and volume), while simultaneously avoiding placental transfer to the fetus. For statistically significant results, at least 8 per group are required (due to hetroginity in tumor growth). The study will include a single time point. Groups:

  1. Huginn-vesicles loaded with chemotherapy.
  2. Biomimetic NP type 1 loaded with chemotherapy.
  3. Biomimetic NP type 2 loaded with chemotherapy.
  4. Biomimetic NP type 3 loaded with chemotherapy.
  5. Free Asgardicin (3 mg/kg).
  6. control group – injecting PBS (as negative control).

Total of mice for experiment 2: 8 mice x 6 groups = 48 mice.

תיאור מהלך הניסוי בבעלי החיים והטיפול בהם

HR-7/Frigg female mice will be housed with HR-7/Frigg males (2 females per male per cage). The presence of vaginal plugs will be checked the following morning as an indication of successful mating. Pregnant females and non-pregnant females will then be transferred to separate cages (males will be house individually). To establish the Triad neoplasm class model, 3-8 × 10^5 Fenrir-4T-Luc cells suspended in 100 μl of PBS will be injected orthotopically into the mammary fat pad of pregnant HR-7/Frigg female mice on gestational day E2.

Experiment 1:

Rune-particles (NPs) (dissolve in 100ul PBS) or Asgardicin (3 mg/kg) or PBS will be administered to pregnant mice on gestational day E16 (tumor day: 7) via intravenous tail vein injection. At 6 and 24 hours post-administration, mice will be anesthetized using ketamine and medetomidine, followed by injecting of Baldur-flare (10 mg/kg) intraperitoneally and imaging 10 minutes post-injection using IVIS After imaging, mice will be euthanized via cervical dislocation, and the uterus will be dissected to collect fetuses and placentas for further imaging. Maternal organs (tumor, liver, placenta) and fetal organs will be harvested for further imaging and analysis.

 

Experiment 2:

Rune-particles (NPs) (dissolve in 100ul PBS) or Asgardicin (3 mg/kg) or PBS will be administered to pregnant mice on gestational day E16 and E18 via intravenous tail vein injection. On E19 (24h post 2ed injection), mice will be anesthetized using ketamine and medetomidine, followed by injecting of Baldur-flare (10 mg/kg) intraperitoneally and imaging 10 minutes post-injection using IVIS After imaging, mice will be euthanized via cervical dislocation, and the uterus will be dissected to collect fetuses and placentas for further imaging. Maternal organs (tumor, liver,

placenta, blood and more) and fetal organs will be harvested for further imaging and analysis.

 

* Male mice will be housed individually due to mating.

* IV injections will be administered via the tail vein. If tail vein access is unsuccessful, retro-orbital injection will be used as an alternative.

* Sacrificing mice will be done using CO2 and cervical dislocation under anesthesia.

* Pups will be sacrifice via decapitation.
