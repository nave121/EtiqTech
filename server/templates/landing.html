Architecture

Three Layers of Review

Layer 1

Deterministic Law Checks

40+ rules aligned with Israeli Prevention of Cruelty to Animals Law (5754-1994). Instant, reproducible results catching missing fields, invalid euthanasia methods, and structural errors.

Layer 2

AI Theme Analysis

An LLM reviews your protocol across 11 ethical dimensions, scoring each 0-3 with detailed rationale. Themes include alternatives search, euthanasia methods, severity classification, personnel training, and more.

Layer 3

Human Eye Review

A holistic 2-pass review using an adversarial expert persona. Pass 1 evaluates each section against a rubric. Pass 2 checks cross-reference consistency. Catches issues that rule-based and theme-based checks miss.
